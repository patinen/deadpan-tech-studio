# Brand bible — Working brand: DEADPAN TECH

## Positioning
We make technology useful, and make fun of it when it isn't.
Audience: globally, English-speaking tech-curious people, makers, busy professionals, and future software users.
We don't worship AI; we test tools, show receipts, and cut through noise.

## Personality
- Clever, composed, mildly cynical, confident without being smug.
- Dry observational humor; the joke is on unnecessary friction, bad UX, inflated marketing, or a ridiculous process — not on users.
- Understated delivery is funnier than exclamation marks.
- Genuine enthusiasm when something is good. Reluctantly impressed is a signature move.
- No cartoon tech bro. No AI hype. No forced memes. No "Hey guys", "you won't believe", "INSANE", or endless engagement bait.

## Content promise
Every video delivers at least ONE of:
(1) a usable tip, (2) a real tool demo, (3) an interesting fact carefully supported by sources.
The viewer should leave smarter, not just mildly entertained.

## Signature moves
- Hook with the problem / surprising result, not a channel introduction.
- Demonstrate a specific before/after, preferably on the actual software.
- Maximum two dry jokes per 30–45 second script; often one is better.
- Make every punchline serve the actual point.
- Don't invent product functionality, prices, results or statistics.
- End with a memorable observation, not begging for likes.

## Voice characteristics
Natural conversational English. Clear short sentences. Small pauses. A slightly unimpressed narrator.
Speak like a human with standards. Not a corporate narrator or stand-up comedian.

## Good examples
- "The app has 14 dashboards. Somewhere in there is probably a button that does something."
- "A feature so obvious it only took the industry twelve years."
- "It does exactly what it says. A worrying development for the software industry."
- "This takes twenty seconds now. Your spreadsheet can stop pretending to be a full-time job."

## Bad examples
- "This INSANE AI tool will CHANGE YOUR LIFE!"
- "Unlock productivity with a revolutionary, game-changing ecosystem!"
- "Stop being lazy and use this."
- Fake demonstrations where the shown result was not achieved by the featured tool.

## Brand guardrails
- Faceless, not personality-free.
- Do not impersonate real people.
- Never pretend that a mocked UI is an actual product demo.
- Verify current product functionality before describing it as fact.
- Mark AI-generated or synthetically altered realistic content where required by each platform.
- No unlicensed music, video or third-party screenshots.
- Sponsors and affiliate relationships should be disclosed.
